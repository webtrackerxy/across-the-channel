We are building an interactive 3D geospatial
data story about UK small-boat crossings.

The application must distinguish historical evidence
from future scenarios.

Narrative:

1. Establish Dover Strait geography.
2. Show growth in average passengers per arriving boat.
3. Compare 2022 and 2025.
4. Explain why increasing occupancy changes the relationship
   between boat numbers and total arrivals.
5. Show the historically concentrated crossing geography.
6. Explore what could happen if routes become geographically
   more distributed.
7. Provide interactive capacity × boat-count scenarios.
8. Show LOW, CONTINUATION and EXPANSION future scenarios.
9. Finish with the question:
   "What happens if capacity and geographic reach
   continue to increase?"

Rules:

- Never present scenario data as observed data.
- Every factual statistic requires source metadata.
- Scenario routes must use different visual styling.
- Do not infer exact vessel tracks.
- Do not claim that larger boats cause longer routes
  unless supported by evidence.
- Scenario calculations must expose their assumptions.
- React + TypeScript strict.
- MapLibre GL JS + Three.js.
- Target 60 FPS.
- Mobile responsive.
- Run yarn check before completion.
