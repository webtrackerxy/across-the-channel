# Methodology

## Historical data

People and boat counts are administrative detections, not estimates of all attempted crossings or unique individuals. People rescued/intercepted and brought to the UK are included; people returned to France by French authorities and undetected arrivals are not. The UK-wide series is not split by departure country.

Average occupancy = people arriving / boats arriving. All calculations use unrounded input counts. Display values are rounded only for presentation. Averages do not describe each individual boat, physical vessel dimensions, safe payload or certified capacity.

The 2022–2025 comparison uses matching full calendar years and a single official summary-table vintage. The provisional 2026 row is separate and explicitly dated. It must not be presented as a forecast or a full-year total.

## Geography

The map is contextual. Its generalised Natural Earth country polygons and manually positioned approximate reference labels do not establish incident coordinates. No exact historical tracks, route distances, dispersion statistics or vessel-range trend are inferred. The research event dataset is incomplete and is not rendered as a representative pattern.

## Estimates and scenarios

Where evidence is absent, this MVP uses explicit hypothetical inputs rather than estimated historical facts. No best-estimate engine, fuel or route-range values are supplied: there is no defensible calibration dataset yet. Scenarios are deterministic illustrations, not forecasts, confidence intervals or likelihood statements.

Source inspection and arithmetic checks have occurred. Independent critical review is pending, as stated in the interface. Research claim approval flags were not changed to imply that review happened.
