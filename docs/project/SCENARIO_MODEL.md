# Scenario model

`Modelled arrivals = assumed arriving boats × assumed average people per boat`.

| Preset | Boats | Occupancy | Result | Geographic illustration |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| LOW | 400 | 45 | 18,000 | Strait |
| CONTINUATION | 670 | 62 | 41,540 | Strait |
| EXPANSION | 800 | 80 | 64,000 | Wider connections |

These are editorial choices for exploration, not estimated future outcomes. CONTINUATION rounds the 2025 counts; LOW and EXPANSION demonstrate lower/higher arithmetic inputs without assigning probabilities. The horizon is one unspecified hypothetical year.

Boat controls range from 0 to 1,500 in steps of ten; occupancy from 1 to 150. These UI bounds are not statements about safe capacity, maximum capacity, historical occurrence or plausibility. Inputs represent arriving boats, not all departures, so no success-rate multiplier is assumed.

The wider-footprint switch changes only illustrative map connections. It cannot change the numeric result. The arcs are dashed and amber, with persistent scenario labels. They do not represent observed or recommended paths, precise destinations, routes around obstacles, navigable tracks or measured distances. Their elevation is visual styling only.

The occupancy/range causal relationship is not modelled. Updating this model requires a separate reviewed hypothesis and supporting data.
