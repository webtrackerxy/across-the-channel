Research the evolution of small-boat crossings from
France to the UK from 2018 to 2026.

The purpose is to build an evidence-based interactive
3D geospatial story.

Research these questions:

1. How many people arrived each year?
2. How many boats arrived each year?
3. What was average occupancy per boat?
4. How has vessel size/capacity changed?
5. Is there evidence that engines, fuel capacity or
   vessel range have changed?
6. Where have documented departure areas been?
7. Where have documented UK arrivals/interceptions occurred?
8. Is there evidence that routes are becoming longer
   or geographically more dispersed?
9. What explanations have UK/French authorities,
   researchers and maritime experts given?
10. What evidence contradicts the hypothesis that
    larger boats are enabling longer routes?

Prioritise sources:

1. UK Home Office
2. National Crime Agency
3. Border Security Command
4. UK Parliament / Commons Library
5. French government / maritime authorities
6. Coastguard / maritime authorities
7. Peer-reviewed research
8. Reuters / BBC / other high-quality reporting

For every claim record:
- claim
- source
- publication date
- URL
- evidence level
- confidence
- geographic location if applicable

Clearly distinguish:
OBSERVED FACT
DERIVED STATISTIC
REPORTED EVENT
HYPOTHESIS
SCENARIO

Do not treat news reports of individual crossings as
evidence of a general trend without supporting data.

Do not begin application development.
